# SABSTCN Viva Quick Demo

1. Open `index.html` and introduce CP3 in DEMO mode.
2. Show L0 Safe, the live clock, sensor cards and health score.
3. Press **SIM L1** for PIR/smoke observation.
4. Press **SIM L2** to show the 5–10 cm proximity zone.
5. Press **SIM L3** for GPS, SMS, alert banner, log row and map pin.
6. Press **SIM L4** for the critical near-object plus smoke condition.
7. Click the log row to focus the permanent evidence pin on the map.
8. Press **GPS LOST** and point out the STORED coordinate badge.
9. Press **HEARTBEAT LOSS** to demonstrate the watchdog and lost-node alert.
10. Switch to LIVE and show where the ESP32 MQTT broker and prefix are configured.
